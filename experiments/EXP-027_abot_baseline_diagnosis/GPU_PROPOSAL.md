## Evaluation gates

At checkpoints 0, 32 and 64, run fixed **unseen-noise** velocity probes on the two fit clips and the already observed dev constant/switch clips, at sigma 0.10/0.25/0.50/0.75/0.90 and two separately frozen probe-noise seeds 113/127. That is 4 clips × 5 sigma × 2 noise × 3 checkpoints = **120 probe DiT forwards**. Probe seeds and states are never passed to optimizer updates. Report per-clip and per-sigma MSE, velocity-delta norm and finite values, not random training-loss trends. Dev results are exploratory, not blind generalization.

The primary learning-signal gate is a **≥10% reduction in held-out-noise mean C2 FM MSE for each fitted clip** at step 64, with improvements present in both low (≤.25) and high (≥.75) sigma bands. This is deliberately above the prior ~1% proxy shifts. If step32 shows no meaningful movement, retain the planned step64 limit rather than add more updates; after step64 stop, regardless of loss, and report PASS/FAIL. If this gate fails, do not spend the optional video stage. If it passes, produce matched FM-8NFE/chunk 56-frame videos for the two fit clips under GT39+C2 generated before/after (4 videos) and one already-observed dev switching clip under self-generated C1+C2 before/after (2 videos). Both checkpoints use the same I0, action spans, seed and initial noise per clip. Review actual character motion, direction, boundary and frame MAD together; lower MAD with less movement does not count as quality recovery. The dev self-history pair is an end-to-end comparison because C1 states differ.
