# EXP-027/v1 · ABot evidence review

**Decision: prioritize a two-clip C2 learnability test before another history-factorial rollout.** The existing results already show that the 33B training hook, gradients, cache protocol and checkpointing work. They do not show that ordinary FM learns a useful action-conditioned correction from four C2 exposures per clip. A history × action-switching grid would currently repeat the same weak checkpoint behavior at substantial inference cost without resolving that uncertainty.

## Data actually available

[DATA_SUMMARY.json](DATA_SUMMARY.json) cross-checks the selected EXP-023 32 train + 2 dev IDs against the earlier action manifest and the existence of their `I0.pt`, `condition.pt` and `full17.pt` files. All 34 encoded triplets exist. The 32 train clips cover four distinct episodes (eight windows each), with 16 constant and 16 switching windows. The two selected dev clips are one constant and one switching clip from two other episodes. Source windows within an episode were previously audited as nonoverlapping; train/dev episode IDs do not overlap. Both dev episodes have already been used for development and are **not blind**. All clips are 56 RGB frames at 24 fps, encoded as 17 latents split into C1=12 and C2=5; the fixed 39-frame prefix/17-frame continuation comparison therefore directly probes C2. Ten train clips have more than one action script in C2, and four change action script exactly between latent 11 and 12. Category `switch` alone does not guarantee an action change at the C1/C2 boundary.

The priority two clips are constant ABot character motions with the same backward key and camera-following description: `43866101158a538d22c52ac5b2fa606b_src0621_rgb56` is **S+D** for all 56 frames and `7199292ca430b478bfd0d83cf2e519d8_src1670_rgb56` is **A+S** for all 56 frames. Thus the lateral command differs while W/S and the camera-following label are similar. They come from different episodes and have different I0, so comparing their generated motion is **not** a same-state A/D causal intervention; they serve as two distinct targets for a learnability check. Their exact 17 action spans and encoded file receipts are in [DATA_SUMMARY.json](DATA_SUMMARY.json).

## Training evidence, separated from fixed evaluation

EXP-023 used a zero-initialized tail8 QKV rank8 adapter, C2-only GT C1 clean-history FM, 128 updates over 32 clips: **four C2 exposures per clip**. Sigma ranged 0.012–0.993; counts in five equal 0.2-wide bins were 30/26/24/28/20. All recorded gradient L2 values were nonzero and finite (range 7.70e-5–7.13e-3). Training loss varied with newly sampled clip, noise and sigma; its first/last windows are not a fixed learning curve. The eight fixed σ=.25/.75 probes fell only **1.23%** on average. Its matched 56-frame videos gave no clear action or visual gain. [EXP-023 report](../EXP-023_v3_fm_small_data/WORKER_REPORT.md).

EXP-025 increased adapter coverage to all 50 QKV blocks (rank8) and trained both C1 and C2 for 256 updates. Each of the same 32 clips still received only **four C1 and four C2 exposures**. Across C1, sigma bins were 36/29/22/20/21; across C2, 22/24/31/27/24. C1 gradient L2 ranged 1.32e-4–2.13e-2, C2 3.04e-4–8.37e-2. [CHECKPOINT_DELTA.json](CHECKPOINT_DELTA.json) shows the trainable weights truly moved: aggregate A delta L2 8.783 and B delta L2 7.325 from zero-initialized B, with all B elements changed. This is parameter movement, not a measure of correct action response. Fixed probes: C1 MSE **+0.23%** overall, C2 **−1.34%** overall; train/dev C2 respectively −1.42%/−1.25%. [EXP-025 metrics](../EXP-025_v3_fm_full_coverage/METRICS_SUMMARY.json).

The [Judge's full-frame review](../EXP-025_v3_fm_full_coverage/judge/VISUAL_REVIEW.md) found recognizable people but little consistent improvement in motion or action. GT-history boundary MAD changed 10.49→10.85; self-history 12.41→9.72 while the self-generated trajectories and movement differed, so lower MAD is not proof of stability. No same-state A/D counterfactual was run, and the two self-history checkpoint videos start from different generated C1 states. The [final Judge decision](../EXP-025_v3_fm_full_coverage/judge/FINAL_REVIEW.md) accepted experiment execution, judged visual/action PARTIAL and did not upgrade V3.

## What the next experiment must resolve

Global sigma coverage and nonzero gradients rule out a wholly inert training path, but neither guarantees enough exposure for any **individual** action-conditioned clip. A two-clip run gives 32 independent-noise C2 exposures per clip at 64 updates, eight times the prior per-clip C2 exposure. The fixed probe must use noise streams never used for updates and several sigma bands; an apparent gain on the training noise alone would only show memorization. If held-out-noise FM error on both train clips falls clearly and videos still do not respond, the issue is more likely objective-to-sampling alignment or generated-history behavior. If even those two targets cannot be fitted despite nonzero adapter updates, repeating larger multi-clip FM runs is low-value; inspect optimization/representation before the history grid. A successful two-clip fit is **local learnability**, not ABot generalization or restored same-state A/D action fidelity.
