1. A large A/D velocity difference is a numerical sensitivity control, not a semantic positive control. It does not establish correct action following by Original-visible. Low cosine means changed sensitivity, not necessarily weakened control; norm reduction and directional change must be reported separately. Existing weak video response and the proposed GT-history state are different conditions.
2. If all three graphs have nonzero differences, H1 is not excluded: magnitude, direction, and behavioral meaning may still differ. If Original-visible has a near-zero difference, this probe is inconclusive, not evidence that causalization cannot matter. Advancing to H2 requires a concrete remaining question, not merely passing a norm threshold.
3. Replicate the reference at each sigma used for a numerical-error threshold, or justify a common error bound. The current two repeats only calibrate sigma 0.25. Any revised call budget must be explicit before execution.
4. Audit full multilayer information paths through action and non-action prefix tokens, not just direct video mask edges. R2 can be described as strict causal only if no current chunk information reaches historical representations indirectly. Current-prefix feedback used in frozen V3 must be distinguished from globally shared prefix feedback removed in this diagnostic.
5. The 0.5 ratio/cosine thresholds are exploratory triage choices. They cannot establish a mechanism, reject all smaller effects, or substitute for behavioral evidence. A further probe must change a research decision; do not repeat the old route audit merely to obtain another changed tensor.
